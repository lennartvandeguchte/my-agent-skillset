## Who
Micro and small apparel brands selling into the EU (a founder plus a few people, sourcing through an
agent or a single factory), who will be required to publish a Digital Product Passport for textiles
once the ESPR textile delegated act lands (expected 2027, with compliance following).

## What they do today instead
They know their tier-1 factory and little else. Fibre origin, dye house, and process data live in the
agent's inbox or nowhere. When asked, they email the factory and receive a PDF or a spreadsheet they
cannot verify and cannot re-use for the next order. The DPP vendor market (Fairlymade, Trustrace,
Retraced, Carbonfact and others) targets brands with sustainability teams and a budget; small brands
either wait, or plan to fill in the minimum with whatever the factory says.

## Why it's unsolved
The delegated act with the exact data fields does not exist yet, so nobody knows the precise minimum.
Suppliers are asked the same questions by every brand separately and answer each one by hand. There is
no cheap way for a brand with no compliance team to collect, keep and re-use supplier answers across
orders. Big-brand tools are priced and designed for big brands.
